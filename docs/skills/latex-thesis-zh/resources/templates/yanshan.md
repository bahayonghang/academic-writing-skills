## Specification information

- **Standard Name**: Yanshan University Graduate Thesis Writing Standards (2024 Edition)
- **Issuing unit**: Yanshan University Degree Office (June 2024)
- **Normative citation**: GB/T 7713.1 (Rules for writing dissertations), GB/T 7714 (Rules for bibliography),
  GB 3100 / GB/T 3101 / GB/T 3102 (amount and unit), GB/T 15835 (number usage), etc. (§2.18.3)

## Obtaining methods and templates

- The college notice usually directs you to download the dissertation template and writing sample in "Graduate Management System → Degree Management → Dissertation Management";
  Download the cover and title page templates from the Graduate School website (§3.3.1).
- If you cannot download it, please contact the Graduate Teaching Office or the Graduate School to confirm the latest version.
- The college may formulate more stringent specific requirements (number of text, number of documents, etc.) based on this specification, giving priority to the college's filing requirements (§2.18.2).

## Key points of content requirements (§1)

- **Title** (§1.1): concise and appropriate, generally no more than 25 words, in both Chinese and English; the total number of subtitle and title should not exceed 35 words,
  Use dashes in Chinese and colons in English; titles should be consistent throughout the text.
- **Abstract** (§1.3.1): independent and self-contained, including purpose, theoretical and practical significance, main research content, research methods, and outstanding results
  and conclusions; generally use the third person ("analyzed..." "believe..."); it is not appropriate to use formulas, chemical structural formulas, charts,
  Abbreviations are rarely used and citation numbers are not indicated.
- **Keywords** (§1.3.2): 3 to 8, arranged from large to small according to the level of denotation. Try to use the standard words of the "Chinese Thesaurus".
- **Introduction** (§1.5.1): Generally Chapter 1, including the source/background/significance of the topic, progress and deficiencies at home and abroad, research framework/content/
  Methods: The main research content should be in the future tense, and avoid using the table of contents as the research content.
- **Each chapter** (§1.5.2): The structure is rigorous and contextual; the data is real and the reasoning is correct; experiments/simulations need to describe conditions and processes;
  Indicate the source when quoting other people's results; there should be a "Summary of the Chapter" after each chapter (chapters such as experimental methods or materials are not required).
- **Conclusion** (§1.5.3): The last chapter of the text, without citing references, generally within 2000 words; doctoral conclusions generally contain the main
  There are three parts: results, innovation points, and prospects. The innovation points are listed in (1) (2) (3). It is appropriate to use "proposed" and "established" to describe and start from
  Evaluation in both qualitative and quantitative aspects; echoes the introduction and does not simply repeat the summary of each chapter; wording is rigorous and avoids "probably" and "maybe"
  "Could be"; limitations or remaining issues should be noted.
- **References** (§1.6): All are marked in order, and only one serial number is used for the same article; try to cite the original literature; Ph.D. candidates are generally
  No less than 100 articles, no less than 40 articles for master's degree, and a certain number of foreign language documents; generally no less than 1/3 of the total number of documents in the past five years,
  And there should be literature from the past two years; textbooks, product manuals, national standards, unpublished reports, etc. are generally not suitable for citation; ** I am studying for a degree.
  Papers published during this period should not be included in the references**.
- **Achievements** (§1.7): Types include academic papers (including accepted ones), monographs/translated works, patents, standards, scientific research awards; and degrees
  Achievements irrelevant to the paper should not be listed.
- **Acknowledgments** (§1.8): The content is simple and the language is implicit.

## Key points of writing requirements (§2)

- **Number of text** (§2.1, including tables and figures): 60,000 to 100,000 words for Ph.D., including about 10,000 words for the introduction; 30,000 to 50,000 words for Master’s degree,
  The introduction should be 3,000 to 5,000 words; for professional degrees, please follow relevant regulations.
- **Abstract word count** (§2.2): 500~650 words for master's degree, 900~1200 words for doctor's degree; English abstract is completely consistent with Chinese, Chinese
  English comes first; keywords are separated by semicolons, and Chinese and English correspond one to one.
- **Table of Contents** (§2.3): Organized to the third level of headings; entries include: Abstract / Abstract / Table of names and symbols of physical quantities (can be omitted) /
  Main text section/References/Appendices (can be omitted)/Achievements achieved during the study of bachelor's degree/Index (can be omitted)/Acknowledgments.
- **Text level** (§2.4): Each chapter starts on a new page; the title is generally within 15 words, and English abbreviations are used as much as possible; the level is generally to level three,
  No more than level four.
- **Citation Mark** (§2.5): GB/T 7714 sequential coding system, upper right corner `[1]`; multiple articles `[7,9]`, continuous `[11-13]`;
When quoting the same document multiple times, add the page number `[3]101-108`; when directly stated in the text, align it with the main text ("it can be known from the literature [8,10-13]");
  **Citations shall not be placed in headings at all levels**.
- **Terms and abbreviations** (§2.6): National standard/industry terminology is adopted and the whole text is unified; the original English word in parentheses is indicated when a non-universal abbreviation appears for the first time.
- **Physical Quantities** (§2.7): Symbols in italics (except for pH); use legal measurement units; unit symbols in upright type; between numerical values and unit symbols
  Leave gaps (except for degrees, minutes and seconds of angles).
- **Numbers** (§2.8, Appendix B): according to GB/T 15835; Arabic numerals use Times New Roman.
- **Formula** (§2.9): Start a new line and center it; arrange the serial number by chapter, such as (1-1), and align the right end; write "see formula (1-1)" for quotation; long formula in
  Break the line after the relation/operation symbols such as =, +, etc., and do not repeat the symbol at the beginning of the following line; the dash "——" in the comment "in the formula" occupies 2 words,
  Line breaks are aligned with the first word after the dash.
- **Insert table** (§2.10): Three-line table (without adding left and right edges), no slash in the table header; number according to chapter "Table 1-1", table title is placed on the table,
  Ph.D. in Chinese and English, M.A. in Chinese only; blank in the table = no item, “—” = tested but not found, 0 = actual measured zero; first in the text
  "See Table 1-1" type prompt; use "Table × (continued)" and repeat the header across pages.
- **Illustrations** (§2.11): Numbered according to the chapter "Figure 1-1", the figure title is placed under the figure, in Chinese and English for PhD, in Chinese only for Master's degree; use (a)(b) for the title of each figure
  And only in Chinese; there is a prompt such as "See Figure 1-1" in the text; figures and figure titles do not split pages; figures in cited literature must be in the upper right corner of figure titles/table titles
  Mark the document serial number.
- **Notes** (§2.13): In the form of footnotes, the serial number of each page starts from ①, the serial number is 4 digits smaller in the upper right corner, and the content is 5 digits in size.
- **Appendix** (§2.14): Numbered consecutively in capital Latin letters (Appendix A, Appendix B…).
- **Result Writing** (§2.15): The format is the same as the reference.
- **Spine** (§2.17): The title and degree-granting unit are marked on the spine of doctoral thesis; the spine is not printed on master's degree thesis.

## Key points in typesetting and printing (§3)

- **Font** (§3.1): Song font for main text, bold font for headings at all levels, Arabic numerals and English Times New Roman.
- **Font size and line spacing** (§3.2):

  | Section | Font size | Before/after paragraph | Line spacing |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Level 1 heading (chapter) | Small size 2 | 1 line / 1 line | 22 pts |
  | Second level heading (section) | Small size 3 | 0.5 line / 0.5 line | 22 pt |
  | L3 heading | Size 4 | 0.5 lines / 0.5 lines | 22 pts |
  | Title IV | Small 4 | 0 / 0 | 22 lbs |
  | Text | Small 4 | 0 / 0 | 22 lbs |
  | References | Size 5 | 0 / 0 | 22 lbs |

  Rest: 22 pounds minimum for sizes 4 and up, 18 pounds for sizes 5 and under.
- **Cover and title page** (§3.3): Download the template from the Graduate School website; the title page contains China National Library Classification Number, UDC, and school code
  10216, confidential; English title page Times New Roman ("A dissertation submitted to Yanshan University...").
- **Page number** (§3.4): Centered below the bottom line of the page; the abstract, table of contents and other leading parts are arranged separately in Roman numerals, and the main text is used
  Arabic numerals; perfect bound, odd-numbered pages at right.
- **Header** (§3.5): Add a header to each page except the title page of the cover; add a thick and thin double line to the top edge of the page (thick line on top, 1.5 points);
  The text is in Song style No. 5; the odd-numbered pages are the number and title of this chapter, and the even-numbered pages are "Yanshan University □□□□ Dissertation".
- **Abstract page** (§3.6): The title is in bold size 2 in the center; "Abstract:" "Keywords:" "Classification number:" is in bold size 4 at the top.
  The content is in Song Dynasty/Times New Roman size 4; the English abstract page corresponds to Title/Abstract/Keywords/Classification.
- **Table of Contents Page** (§3.7): Level 1 entries are in bold font size 4, Level 2 and below are in Song font size 4, number symbols are Times New Roman,
  1.2 times line spacing, wrap beyond the next line and indent to the first character of the title.
- **Text level** (§3.8): The first level is "Chapter 1 □□" (numbered with Arabic numerals, centered); the second level starts with "1.1" in the top grid;
  The number and title of each level must not be placed on the last line of the page.
- **Printing and binding** (§3.9): A4 (210 mm×297 mm); page margins top and bottom 3.0 cm, left and right 2.8 cm; header and footer margins
  Border 2.5 cm; printed on both sides starting from the Chinese abstract; Table of Contents page and Chapter 1 starting on the right page; Image printed in color; Hot glue bound
  (No metal nails allowed).

## Blind review

> R1/R2 is the school’s blind review notice **original** (included verbatim and will not be rewritten); R3 is common practice (not the original text of the school’s notice),
>Subject to the school’s blind review notice. For the inspection and blind review version generation process, see `references/modules/blind-review.md`
> (`blind-review` module, spec-check is run again before final inspection and before submission).

- **R1 (original text of school notice)**: Hide the names of authors and tutors, acknowledgments, etc.;
- **R2 (original notice from the school)**: In the "Achievements Obtained During the Degree Study" section, delete the name, achievement titles and journal page numbers, leaving only the order of my signature, journal name and year. Such as: `[1]第一作者，机械工程学报，2024`.

R2 rewriting example (before and after rewriting; names/titles are fictitious placeholders, only for demonstration format):

- Before rewriting: `[1] 张三, 李四, 王五. 某某机构的某某研究[J]. 机械工程学报, 2024, 60(5): 100-110.`
- After rewriting: `[1] 第一作者，机械工程学报，2024`

R3 extended hiding point (**common practice, subject to the school’s blind review notice**): originality statement/authorization instructions signature page,
Cover and title page personal fields, English title page By/Supervisor line, fund project number,
Self-referential identity statements in the text (such as "the author is in the ××× research group").

The blind review version generates red lines: only write `*_blind`, the copy and original file bytes remain unchanged; `\cite`/`\ref`/`\label`/mathematical environment
Keep verbatim; zero rewriting of the result entry script (only insert `% TODO-BLIND(R2)` comments, suggestions given by agent to `[LLM]`,
After the user confirms, the copy will be dropped and the signature order will not change).

## Academic misconduct detection (provisional regulations in 2011, need to confirm whether they have been updated)

- Detection system: CNKI TMLC (dissertation academic misconduct detection system).
- Scope of detection: The main text of the paper; abstracts, references, acknowledgments, etc. are not included.
- Text copy ratio judgment: ≤20% passes; 20%~50% needs to be rechecked after modification; >50% fails.
- Please refer to the latest notice from the Graduate School. If there are new regulations, you should follow them first.
