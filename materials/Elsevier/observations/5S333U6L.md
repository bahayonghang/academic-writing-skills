## Structure

数字节：`1. Introduction` → `2. Related work` → `3. Methodology` → `4. Results and discussion` → `5. Conclusion`。前置 `ABSTRACT` 与 `Keywords`。独立 Related Work（`2. Related work`，机理模型 vs 深度学习，再 `2.3. Novelty of the proposed DABP framework`）。Introduction 末有编号贡献 + 节序路标，指向 Section II–V。Method 标题为 `Methodology`。Experiments 标题为 `Results and discussion`（六公开基准 + FCC 工业数据 + 消融）。

## Openers

- abstract: `Industrial product output` — "Industrial product output prediction and quality monitoring are crucial for resource planning and sustainable development."
- introduction: `In the industrial` — "In the industrial production, accurate prediction of the product output and quality plays an important role in the stable development of enterprises and the improvement of market competitiveness (Wang et al., 2025a)."
- method: `This section details` — "This section details the proposed Dynamically Tuned Attention-Based Backpropagation Neural Network (DABP) for industrial soft sensing."
- experiments: `Experimental results on` — "Experimental results on the six benchmark datasets confirm the proposed DABP model superiority over the six comparative models across all evaluated metrics."
- conclusion: `This study has` — "This study has successfully developed a DABP for soft sensing in industrial processes, with a specific application to product yield prediction and quality monitoring in a Fluid Catalytic Cracking (FCC) unit."

## Hedge verbs

- propose / causal / abstract, introduction: "this paper proposes"; "A novel soft sensing framework of the DABP is proposed"
- demonstrate / causal / abstract, introduction, conclusion: "demonstrating its strong potential"; "The results demonstrate the superior predictive capability"
- confirm / causal / experiments, conclusion: "Experimental results ... confirm"; "its efficacy is confirmed on a real-world industrial FCC dataset"
- show / associative / conclusion: "the DABP model shows significant practical potential"
- plan / speculative / conclusion: "First, we plan to integrate intelligent optimization algorithms"
