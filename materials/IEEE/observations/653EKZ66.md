## Structure

罗马数字标题：`I. INTRODUCTION` → `II. METHOD` → `III. EXPERIMENTS` → `IV. CONCLUSION` → `V. FUTURE WORK`。前置 `Abstract—` 与 `Index Terms—`。无独立 Related Work。`related_work=inlined`（Introduction 中段评 model-based/signal-based、深度故障诊断、对抗/子域自适应、视觉域 TTA：TENT/T3A/CoTTA）。Introduction 末有节序路标，指向 II–V。Conclusion 与 Future Work 分节。Method 在正文中段。

## Openers

- abstract: `Cross-domain bearing` — "Cross-domain bearing fault diagnosis algorithms have garnered considerable attention in recent years due to their robust ability to address domain bias." (p.107)
- introduction: `FAULT diagnosis constitutes` — "FAULT diagnosis constitutes a critical component of prediction and health management, with precise diagnostics contributing to enhanced equipment reliability and production safety." (p.107；栏首掉字)
- method: `We tackle the` — "We tackle the challenge of unsupervised cross-domain bearing fault diagnosis in the absence of source domain data." (p.109, II.A)
- experiments: `Our experiments were` — "Our experiments were conducted on two datasets: the Paderborn University (PU) Dataset [28], the PHM Data Challenge on 2009 (PHM2009) Dataset [29], and the SEU Dataset [30]." (p.111)
- conclusion: `This article introduces` — "This article introduces an online test-time domain adaptation algorithm designed to tackle the challenges of machine intelligence fault diagnosis in real-world scenarios with unknown data distribution." (p.116)

Future Work 首句："Our approach specifically tackles the challenge of adaptation across operating conditions but does not extend to addressing fault detection across devices." (p.116)。不单列 `related_work` opener。

## Gap transitions

- however (abstract): "However, prevailing methods often grapple with two key challenges: the absence of privacy preservation (necessitating access to source domain data) and the inability to facilitate real-time predictions (requiring iterative training on complete target domain data)." (p.107)
- in response (abstract): "In response to these issues, this article introduces an algorithm designed to adapt a pretrained model to the target domain in an online fashion." (p.107)
- however (introduction): "However, the impressive performance of these methods is often confined to similar or identical data distributions and relies heavily on training with a large amount of labeled data." (p.107)
- in response (introduction): "In response to this critical challenge, researchers have developed a range of transfer learning-based methods [14], [15], [16]." (p.107)
- to address (introduction): "To address these pressing issues, we introduce a pioneering online cross-domain fault diagnosis algorithm tailored for the real-time diagnosis of target domain machines." (p.108)
- unlike (introduction): "Unlike classical cross-domain algorithms, our approach excels in situations where source domain data are inaccessible for adaptation." (p.108)

## Hedge verbs

- introduce / causal / abstract, introduction, conclusion: "this article introduces an algorithm"; "we introduce a pioneering online cross-domain fault diagnosis algorithm"; "This article introduces an online test-time domain adaptation algorithm"
- propose / causal / introduction: "We propose an online test-time domain adaption algorithm"; "We propose utilizing perturbed data augmentation"; "we propose to store target domain features"
- demonstrate / causal / abstract, introduction, experiments: "Evaluation on several public datasets demonstrates"; "Our proposed method demonstrates a substantial improvement"; "The results presented in Table IV clearly demonstrate"

## Cross-section linkers

- introduction → method: "The subsequent organization of the article is as follows. In Section II, we elaborate on the problem setting and the method proposed in this article. Section III validates the effectiveness and robustness of the method. Section IV summarizes the article. Finally, Section V discusses the limitations of the article and suggests future research directions." (p.108)
- method → experiments: 框图段落后直接 `III. EXPERIMENTS` (p.111)
- experiments → conclusion: 运行时间段落后直接 `IV. CONCLUSION`，再接 `V. FUTURE WORK` (p.116)

## Candidate rules

- R001 摘要用 `this article introduces` 而非 `Here we`。
- R002 Related Work 并入 Introduction，无独立 RELATED WORK。
- R003 Introduction 末用 `The subsequent organization of the article is as follows`（变体，非 `The rest of this article`）。
- R004 贡献用 `Our contributions can be succinctly summarized as follows.` + 编号列表。
- R005 局限与展望单列 `V. FUTURE WORK`，结论节本身不写 `Although`。

## Candidate phrases

- `In response to these issues, this article introduces` (abstract)
- `To address these pressing issues, we introduce` (introduction)
- `Our contributions can be succinctly summarized as follows.` (introduction)
- `The subsequent organization of the article is as follows.` (introduction)
- `This article introduces an online test-time domain adaptation algorithm` (conclusion)

## House style

自称 `this article introduces` / `we introduce` / `We propose` / `our method` / `our approach`。未见 `Here we`、`In this paper`。进 phrase_bank，不进 anti_ai_patterns。
