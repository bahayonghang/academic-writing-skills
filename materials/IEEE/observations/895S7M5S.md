RELATED WORK`，含数据驱动质量预测与 MOEL/ENAS）。`related_work=independent`。Introduction 末有节序路标，指向 Section II–V。Method 在 III。Experiments 标题为 `EXPERIMENTAL STUDY`（基准 + CAPL 带钢）。

- show / causal / abstract, experiments: "Experimental results on both benchmark data and practical data of continuous annealing show that MOCNNEL-MSDF achieves competitive or better accuracy"

- conclude / causal / experiments: "Therefore, it can be concluded that the proposed MOCNNEL-MSDF achieves better performance in terms of accuracy and robustness against the comparison learning methods."

## Candidate rules

- R001 abstract 用 `To address this issue` + 方法缩写 `is developed`，不用 `we propose`。
- R002 有独立 `II. RELATED WORK`，先质量预测再 MOEL/ENAS。
