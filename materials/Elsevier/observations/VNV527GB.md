## Structure

数字节：`1. Introduction` → `2. Method and data` → `3. Results` → `4` 讨论（`4.2. Perspective`）→ `5. Conclusion`。前置 `highlights` / `graphical abstract` / `article info` / `Keywords` / `abstract`。无独立 Related Work。`related_work=inlined`（Introduction 中段评 LEAP/CSC/AIM、协同控制与 CEA）。Introduction 末用 `In this study, we use CEA` 收束目标，无编号贡献列表，也无独立节序路标句。Method 在 `Method and data`。Experiments 标题为 `Results`（技术评价 + 路线图 + 情景）。

## Openers

- abstract: `Cement is a` — "Cement is a critical material for urbanization, but its energy-intensive production creates serious potential environmental impacts."
- introduction: `China has been` — "China has been the largest cement producer for years, accounting for ∼60% of global production [1]."
- method: `The research scope` — "The research scope of the study is the entire life-cycle process of cement production, from limestone mining to cement finish grinding (details about cement production and air emissions are shown in Fig. A.1)." (s.2.1)
- experiments: `The results show` — "The results show that a number of technologies have a high multi-pollutant abatement capacity, which is illustrated by the concentrated red shadings in Table 1." (s.3.1)
- conclusion: `The aim of` — "The aim of this study is to establish a cost-effective technology roadmap for Chinese cement industry to maximize the co-benefits of pollution mitigation and energy conservation."

## Gap transitions

- however (introduction): "However, the problem arises as the pollutant mitigation and energy saving effects of technologies differ significantly or even conflict with each other."
- moreover (introduction): "Moreover the comparison between different technologies, especially those applied in the same production process is still lacking at present."
- to solve (introduction): "To solve the problem, the cost-effectiveness of these technologies should be assessed comprehensively."
- in order to (introduction): "In order to bridge the gap between technology providers, technology users and policy makers, more efforts need to be exerted to evaluating available technologies as well as to balance the conflicting effect of them."
- although (results): "Although the intensely promoted waste heat recovery for power generation (x05) has a distinct energy saving effect, its capital cost is somewhat high at 8.4 Yuan per ton clinker and thus the FNPV is not remarkable."

## Hedge verbs

- conduct / causal / abstract: "In this study we conducted a cost-effectiveness analysis"
- indicate / associative / abstract: "The finding indicates the necessity to design the technology promotion roadmap."
- prove / causal / abstract: "it proves to be the best solution for achieving energy saving, PM2.5, SO2 and CO2 abatement compared to single-objective optimization models"
- show / causal / experiments: "The results show that a number of technologies have a high multi-pollutant abatement capacity"
- confirm / causal / conclusion: "The results confirm that technology promotion measure is an economically and environmentally efficient strategy for cement industry"

## Cross-section linkers

- introduction → method: 引言末 `In this study, we use CEA to comprehensively evaluate... Finally, we conduct a scenario analysis...` 后直接 `2. Method and data`，无 `The remainder of this paper` 句。
- method → experiments: 数据来源后 `3. Results`
- experiments → conclusion: `4.2. Perspective` 后 `5. Conclusion`
