## Structure

罗马数字标题：`I. INTRODUCTION` → `II. TECHNICAL BACKGROUND` → `III. PROPOSED PREDICTION FRAMEWORK` → `IV. CASE STUDY` → `V. RESULT AND DISCUSSION` → `VI. CONCLUSION`。前置 `Abstract—` 与 `Index Terms—`。无独立 Related Work。`related_work=inlined`（Introduction 中段评 RNN、hybrid CNN-LSTM、PCA/VMD/EWT 去噪）。Introduction 末有 `The remainder of this article is organized as follows` 路标，指向 Section II–VI。Method 在 III。Experiments 拆成 `CASE STUDY` 与 `RESULT AND DISCUSSION`。
