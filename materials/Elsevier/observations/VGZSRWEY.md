- despite (introduction): "Despite several efforts, such as increasing the new dry process application, closing obsolete plants, and using various best practice technologies, that have been made by Chinese government in the past two decades, recent studies indicate that there is still large opportunity to improve energy efficiency, reduce emissions of GHGs and air pollutants [7–10]."
