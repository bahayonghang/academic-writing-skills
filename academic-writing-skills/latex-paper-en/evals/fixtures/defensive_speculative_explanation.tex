\section{Discussion Cases}

\subsection{Case A: Unsupported mechanism stack}
The intervention improved macro-F1 by 3.2 points. This gain may arise because the
auxiliary loss smooths the optimization landscape, because the encoder learns more stable
features, or because the decoder becomes better calibrated. The present comparison did not
measure any of these mechanisms and cannot verify which, if any, produced the gain.

\subsection{Case B: Single hedged explanation with local evidence}
The lower error may reflect reduced prediction variance: across ten seeds, variance fell by
18\%, and removing the ensemble restored the original variance.

\subsection{Case C: Multiple hypotheses with evidence and a discriminator}
Two mechanisms contributed to the improvement. Class-balanced augmentation increased
rare-class recall by 6.1 points without changing calibration, whereas temperature scaling
reduced expected calibration error from 0.082 to 0.041 without changing recall. Separate
ablations distinguished the contribution of each mechanism.

\subsection{Case D: Mechanism explicitly undetermined}
The intervention improved macro-F1 by 3.2 points. The current design does not identify the
mechanism responsible for this difference.

\subsection{Case E: Strong claim earned by a controlled experiment}
In the randomized ablation, disabling retrieval removed the full 3.2-point gain while all
other components and seeds were held fixed. Within this benchmark, retrieval caused the
observed improvement.
