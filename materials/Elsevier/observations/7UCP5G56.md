## Gap transitions

- to address (introduction): "To address data scarcity challenges, virtual sample generation (VSG) techniques have emerged as a promising solution through intelligent synthesis of reliable samples [5][6]."
- however (introduction): "However, such methods demonstrated limited efficacy in handling complex nonlinear systems and capturing true dynamic behaviors."
- nevertheless (introduction): "Nevertheless, GAN implementations in industrial settings remain constrained by training instability and mode collapse phenomena, particularly when handling complex system dynamics."
- in summary (introduction): "In summary, traditional methodologies reliably ensure process constraint satisfaction in sample generation, yet their reliance on local neighborhood search mechanisms proves inadequate for modeling nonlinear interdependencies within high-dimensional feature spaces."
- to bridge (introduction): "To bridge these gaps, our study introduces a two-stage metaheuristic framework (MVSG-GS) addresses data scarcity in process modeling through correlation-aware virtual sample generation."
