# State Management

State here is the writing workflow state that the skill asks the agent to keep
while revising a paper.

Local pattern:

- Start with a mini-outline before drafting prose.
- Keep one paragraph for one message.
- Track the paragraph topic sentence, evidence points, and thesis mapping during
  reverse outlining.
- Preserve a claim-evidence map for major claims.
- Keep terminology stable across the whole paper.

Reference files:

- `ref/Research-Paper-Writing-Skills/research-paper-writing/SKILL.md`
- `ref/Research-Paper-Writing-Skills/research-paper-writing/references/does-my-writing-flow-source.md`
- `ref/Research-Paper-Writing-Skills/research-paper-writing/references/paper-review.md`

Avoid:

- Rewriting paragraph order without rechecking the outline.
- Letting one section's terminology drift away from the rest of the paper.
- Revising sentences without updating the claim-evidence map.
