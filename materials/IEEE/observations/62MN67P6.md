## Structure

罗马数字标题：`I. INTRODUCTION` → `II. BACKGROUND` → `III. METHOD` → `IV. CASE STUDIES` → `V. CONCLUSION`。前置 `Abstract—` 与 `Index Terms—`。无独立 Related Work。`related_work=inlined`（Introduction 中段评 mean/median、PCA、kNN、soft-impute、SVM、DAE、GAN、CNN、MR-PVAE、Multirate-Former、BRITS）。II 为 `BACKGROUND`（DAE 预备）。Introduction 贡献列表后直接进入 II，无 `The rest of this article is organized as follows`。Method 在 III。Experiments 标题为 `CASE STUDIES`（debutanizer + hydrocracking）。

## Openers

- abstract: `Missing values are` — "Missing values are a common occurrence in industrial datasets, resulting from multiple sampling rates, sensor malfunctions, and transmission errors, whose presence can significantly affect the accuracy of data-driven models." (p.1)
- introduction: `N recent years` — "N recent years, with the advent of the big-data era, data-driven process monitoring, fault diagnosis, fault classification, and soft sensors have become areas of strong research interest [1-4]." (p.1；栏首掉字，应为 IN)
- method: `A denoising auto-encoder` — "A denoising auto-encoder is usually used to obtain complex and abstract feature representations that can improve the performance of missing data imputation [35]." (p.3, III)
- experiments: `The missing-data completion` — "The missing-data completion performance of PE-DAE+SPL is evaluated by the debutanizer column and the hydrocracking process." (p.5, IV)
- conclusion: `In this paper` — "In this paper, a position-encoding denoising auto-encoder (PE-DAE) is proposed for missing value imputation." (p.10)

## Gap transitions

- however (introduction): "However, the success of data-driven methods depends heavily on the quality of the process data." (p.1)
- however (introduction): "However, during the training process, nonmissing values in the incomplete samples are excluded." (p.1)
- however (introduction): "However, most of these methods initialize missing values by replacing values at missing positions with the mean of nonmissing values for each variable." (p.2)

- despite (conclusion): "Despite the promising results, the study's limitations include the difficulty of selecting optimal parameters for SPL." (p.10)

## Hedge verbs

- propose / causal / abstract, introduction, conclusion: "This paper proposes a new position-encoding denoising auto-encoder (PE-DAE)"; "a self-paced learning (SPL) training strategy is proposed"
- show / causal / abstract, conclusion: "Comparative experiments show that the PE-DAE+SPL achieves the smallest error at all missing rates"; "The results show that PE-DAE outperforms"
- can / speculative / abstract: "The SPL training framework can first learn the knowledge structure of data with low missing rates"
- should / speculative / conclusion: "Future research should focus on developing automated and efficient methods for SPL parameter tuning."

## Cross-section linkers

- method → experiments: SPL 段落后 `IV. CASE STUDIES` (p.5)

## Candidate rules

- R001 abstract 用 `This paper proposes a new` + 方法缩写，结论同样用 `In this paper`。
- R002 Introduction 无独立 Related Work，也无 `The rest of this article is organized as follows`；贡献用 `The main contributions of this article are summarized as follows.`（article 与 paper 混用）。
- R003 Method 节标题为单数 `METHOD`，实验节为 `CASE STUDIES`。
- R005 Conclusion 先收回方法，再用 `Despite the promising results, the study's limitations include`，最后 `Future research should focus on`。
