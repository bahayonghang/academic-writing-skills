We are submitting our manuscript, "Latency-Aware Gating for Streaming Anomaly Detection," for consideration at the Journal of Streaming Systems.

The paper asks a narrow but practical question: can a pretrained detector skip computation when its early layers are already confident? We show that a small learned gate cuts inference latency by 47% on SWaT-Stream without hurting F1. On WADI-Stream the same gate matches the strongest baseline while running about twice as fast.

We were careful not to overreach. The gains hold on the two benchmarks we tested, and we say so plainly in the discussion. We do not argue that the method generalizes beyond streaming sensor data.

Two colleagues who work on industrial monitoring read an early draft and thought the deployment notes would interest your readers. That is why we are sending it here rather than to a more theory-focused venue.

The manuscript is original, is not under review elsewhere, and all authors have approved it. We declare no competing interests.

Thank you for considering our work.
