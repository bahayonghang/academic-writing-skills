## Structure

数字节：`1. Introduction` → `2. Related work` → `3. Theoretical background` → `4. Anomaly detection framework` → `5. Performance evaluations` → `6. Ablation experiments` → `7. Conclusion`。前置 `ABSTRACT`、`ARTICLE INFO` / `Keywords`。有独立 Related Work。`related_work=independent`。Introduction 末有编号贡献 + 节序路标。Method 拆成理论背景与异常检测框架。Experiments 标题为 `Performance evaluations`，其后独立 `Ablation experiments`。
