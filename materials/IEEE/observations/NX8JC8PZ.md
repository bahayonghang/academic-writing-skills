## Candidate rules

- R001 abstract 用 `This article proposes`，缺口后再用 `Therefore, a ... method is proposed`。
- R002 Introduction 无独立 Related Work；预备节给过程与算法背景。
- R003 Introduction 末用 `The rest of this article is organized as follows` 指向 II–VI。
- R004 贡献用 `the main contributions of this article are summarized as follows.` + 编号列表。
- R005 Conclusion 用过去时 `was proposed`，再用 `However` 承认局限，`The future works will focus on` 指向后续。
